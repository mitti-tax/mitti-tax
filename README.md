### About me

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at Murdoch University (Bachelor of IT, majoring in Computer Science), graduating in late 2026. I build full-stack web apps, self-host infrastructure at home, and tinker with hardware and local LLMs for fun. Before switching into CS, I spent a year and a half in a Bachelor of Molecular Sciences / Master of Bioinformatics at UWA.

I recently completed a Junior Developer internship at **Imvelo Pty Ltd**, contributing to a live enterprise application (React, TypeScript, C#/.NET), including UI work on optimisation dashboards and export tooling for GeoTIFF, DXF, and CSV formats.

- 🎓 Final-year BIT (Computer Science) @ Murdoch University
